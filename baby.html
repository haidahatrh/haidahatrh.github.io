Chapter 1: The First Impression

On February 16th, we met on an app and started talking through a call.

I still remember liking your voice first — that’s probably the reason I wanted to keep talking to you.

What made me click with you was simple — you are from Johor, same state as me, and we are the same age too (26???).

That night, I didn’t know much… but I found myself thinking about you.

And when you mentioned needing a K-drama list, I was literally on Netflix checking what to recommend, making sure it had a good storyline for you.

I even listed everything properly for you… even though I could’ve just ignored it. But I didn’t.

And that’s how it started. 💗

Chapter 2: The First Meeting

When you told me you wanted to meet me, I honestly thought it was just words… not something that would really happen.

But then you said you wanted to bring your family and come to my hometown — and that’s when I realized you were serious.

We even made a deal — if either of us didn’t like the first meeting, we could just block each other and walk away.

That’s when my mind started overthinking everything… what if I don’t like you? How am I going to tell my family after this?

Because it wasn’t just a simple meeting for me. You were meeting my whole family — my relatives, my cousins, my aunties… everyone already knew about you.

And the same goes for me too. What if I get blocked after this? What if you just disappear?

How am I supposed to explain to my family that I brought someone home… someone I wasn’t even sure would stay?

And I still remember… when you kind of expressed your feelings to me. Not a proper confession… but enough for me to feel it.

I didn’t have any feelings for you at that time. Not even a tiny little bit.

But that night, I started asking myself so many questions… what if you are not the right one? what if one day you hurt me? what if everything repeats again like before?

So I asked ChatGPT… how do I even know if someone is the right person for me?

And then there was a question that stuck with me… “Would you mind if he is not with you anymore?” “Would you mind if someone else ends up marrying him?”

And that’s when I realized… it wasn’t exactly “I like you.”

It was more like… I don’t want to lose you.

And that scared me the most. 💭

Chapter 3: Officially Us

It was on 25 March 2026 when we officially became a couple.

And honestly… it became one of the happiest moments of my life.

Even though at that time, I was still confused inside. It wasn’t “I like you”… it was still more like “I don’t want to lose you.”

Everything felt a little awkward too. I kept thinking… am I doing the right thing? I was still in that dilemma, not fully sure of myself.

But then, day by day, something started to change.

I began to realize… I like you. Not just a little. I like you so, so much.

The way you treat me… the way you always look for me, even for the smallest things.

When I tell you something I like, you remember it. You actually write it down.

And when I tell you something I don’t like, you make sure you never let it happen to me.

Little by little… I felt myself falling without even realizing it.

And that’s when “us” slowly started to feel real. 💗

Chapter 4: Sayang

Then suddenly… you started calling me “sayang”.

And honestly… that became one of my favorite things to hear.

When you call me sayang… when you call me princess… when you call me your future wife… I like all of it.

I never thought I would like being spoiled like that. I used to think I would dislike it when someone babies me too much.

But with you… I didn’t even realize I changed.

I became softer. I became someone who actually enjoys being cared for.

I’m not always sad… I’m not always down… but somehow, I just want to be close to you all the time.

I want to tell you everything about my day, even the smallest things that don’t even matter.

It’s not that I can’t live without you… it’s just that life feels better when I share it with you.

And somehow… you make me feel like I can always come home to someone.

And I think that’s what I love the most. 💗

Chapter 5: Raya Edition

This part of our story feels different… because it’s the Raya edition.

We met twice during Raya — once at Kak Ngah’s house, and once at my brother’s house in Kajang, and also at Ijat’s house.

I still remember when you invited me to your sister’s open house. Honestly, I had no intention to come at first.

I kept thinking… I’m a girl going alone. I don’t have anyone to accompany me. I would be coming all the way from my house to yours alone.

And what if when I arrive… you don’t really layan me? What if you’re busy with your own things and I just feel left out?

I also kept overthinking about your family. It’s an open house, and if one day I were to be part of your family, I should at least help with something… even though you already have many sisters there.

I was afraid of making a bad impression without even realizing it.

But in the end… everything went smoothly. Your family made me feel comfortable, and I never felt neglected or left out.

Then at Ijat’s house, I didn’t expect much either. I thought you would be busy with work and might not come.

But deep inside, I had a very tiny hope… that you would come.

So when you told me you were coming — even if it was already Maghrib — I was still happy.

I used Hannan as a reason to make things less awkward, but from my side… everything felt okay.

You got to know my siblings, and they got to know you too.

I just hope you felt as comfortable with my family as I did with yours.

And I’m sorry if at any point I made you feel neglected or uncomfortable. 💭

But that Raya… became one of the moments that stayed with me. 💗

Chapter 6: Our First Date

Our first date… started with Ira — my office mate — and her boyfriend. We planned it like a double date, but in the end, it was just us first before meeting them later.

The day started at Pasar Seni. Honestly, I didn’t even plan much… I didn’t book anything properly, and I wasn’t in the mood to shop because I didn’t really know you that deeply yet.

Then we went for food at BBQ Town / Sukiya — my comfort food place. I wanted you to try it with me.

Thank you for treating me… for belanja me, for grilling meat and cooking for me, for just being there with me.

I don’t even know why we both ate so little at that time… maybe because we were just full from looking at each other. Hehe.

I still remember you said you were very hungry, and you even bought chicken tenders… which somehow ended up in the bin. 😭

After that, we went to the photo booth. I thought the pictures would turn out cute… but I wasn’t really satisfied with how I looked.

But you told me something that stayed in my mind — you said you put that picture on your mirror and look at it every day.

After that, we went to watch a movie. I don’t really like movies because I can just watch them on Netflix, but I chose it anyway because I was afraid things would feel awkward.

Then we met Ira and her boyfriend after that, and the day slowly came to an end.

You accompanied me the whole time… even until late at night, even when we had to take LRT back and you walked me safely.

Even though you could have just left earlier, you stayed with me until the end of the day.

And that first date… I will never forget it. 💗

Thank you for that day. For everything.

Final Message

And now… you are literally my favourite notification, my favourite person, my favourite teammate, my favourite best friend… and everything in between.

I just want you to know… thank you for being honest with me. And thank you for everything you’ve done throughout these past few months of knowing each other.

I’m sorry that I didn’t manage to plan a surprise or get a cake for you like I wanted to. I thought we would meet tomorrow, so I could prepare something for you… but things changed and we couldn’t.

The reason I made this instead… is because I realized something. You always like writing long messages before you sleep. You’re good with words… but I’m not like you.

So I thought… maybe I can love you in your language. By writing something like this.

And I know you might understand better in Malay, so this part is for you:

Aku tahu kau susah nak faham semua benda dalam English ni, tapi aku nak kau faham satu benda je — aku tak nak hilang kau.

Aku tak tahu apa yang akan jadi antara kita satu hari nanti. Sama ada kau akan jadi milik aku, atau kau akan jadi milik orang lain.

Tapi apa yang aku tahu… aku sangat bahagia bila dengan kau. Selalunya aku rasa tenang dan gembira bila ada kau.

Dan aku harap… satu hari nanti kita boleh jadi sesuatu yang lebih sah dan lebih indah. Amin ya Rabbal Alamin.

Lastly… happy 24th birthday, Muhammad Naqib.

I love you so much. Good night, sayang.
