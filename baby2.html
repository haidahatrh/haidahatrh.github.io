✦ Chapter One ✦

The First Impression

On February 16th, we met on an app and started talking through a call.

I still remember liking your voice first — that's probably the reason I wanted to keep talking to you.

What made me click with you was simple — you are from Johor, same state as me, and we are the same age too (26???).

That night, I didn't know much… but I found myself thinking about you.

And when you mentioned needing a K-drama list, I was literally on Netflix checking what to recommend, making sure it had a good storyline for you.

I even listed everything properly for you… even though I could've just ignored it. But I didn't.

And that's how it started. 💗

✦ Chapter Two ✦

The First Meeting

When you told me you wanted to meet me, I honestly thought it was just words… not something that would really happen.

But then you said you wanted to bring your family and come to my hometown — and that's when I realized you were serious.

We even made a deal — if either of us didn't like the first meeting, we could just block each other and walk away.

That's when my mind started overthinking everything… what if I don't like you? How am I going to tell my family after this?

"Would you mind if he is not with you anymore? Would you mind if someone else ends up marrying him?"

And that's when I realized… it wasn't exactly "I like you." It was more like… I don't want to lose you.

And that scared me the most. 💭

✦ Chapter Three ✦

Officially Us

It was on 25 March 2026 when we officially became a couple.

And honestly… it became one of the happiest moments of my life.

Even though at that time, I was still confused inside. It wasn't "I like you"… it was still more like "I don't want to lose you."

But then, day by day, something started to change. I began to realize… I like you. Not just a little. I like you so, so much.

The way you treat me… the way you always look for me, even for the smallest things.

When I tell you something I like, you remember it. You actually write it down.

Little by little… I felt myself falling without even realizing it.

And that's when "us" slowly started to feel real. 💗

✦ Chapter Four ✦

Sayang

Then suddenly… you started calling me "sayang". And honestly… that became one of my favorite things to hear.

When you call me sayang… when you call me princess… when you call me your future wife… I like all of it.

I never thought I would like being spoiled like that. I used to think I would dislike it when someone babies me too much.

But with you… I didn't even realize I changed. I became softer. I became someone who actually enjoys being cared for.

I want to tell you everything about my day, even the smallest things that don't even matter.

It's not that I can't live without you… it's just that life feels better when I share it with you.

And somehow… you make me feel like I can always come home to someone.

And I think that's what I love the most. 💗

✦ Chapter Five ✦

Raya Edition

We met twice during Raya — once at Kak Ngah's house, and once at my brother's house in Kajang, and also at Ijat's house.

I still remember when you invited me to your sister's open house. I kept thinking… I'm a girl going alone. What if when I arrive… you don't really layan me?

But in the end… everything went smoothly. Your family made me feel comfortable, and I never felt neglected or left out.

Then at Ijat's house, deep inside, I had a very tiny hope… that you would come. So when you told me you were coming — even if it was already Maghrib — I was still happy.

You got to know my siblings, and they got to know you too. I just hope you felt as comfortable with my family as I did with yours.

But that Raya… became one of the moments that stayed with me. 💗

✦ Chapter Six ✦

Our First Date

Our first date started at Pasar Seni. Then we went for food at BBQ Town / Sukiya — my comfort food place. I wanted you to try it with me.

Thank you for treating me… for grilling meat and cooking for me, for just being there with me.

After that, we went to the photo booth. You told me something that stayed in my mind — you said you put that picture on your mirror and look at it every day.

You accompanied me the whole time… even until late at night, even when we had to take LRT back and you walked me safely.

Even though you could have just left earlier, you stayed with me until the end of the day.

And that first date… I will never forget it. 💗

Thank you for that day. For everything.
