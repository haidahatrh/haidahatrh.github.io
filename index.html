Thank you — V-HUB

A full point-of-sale system for a chain of building-materials outlets, covering both walk-in retail and credit-managed contractor purchases. It's designed around the daily rhythm of a Malaysian branch — morning cashier declaration, held bills across the day, closing variance at night.

Under a single admin hub sit seven modules covering the whole floor: POS for sales, RA for order tickets and the panel calculator, CMF for cashier & cash flow, IFS for pickup fulfilment, SCD for stock cards, BSM for cross-branch monitoring, and Admin for the back-office. A parallel Training Mode with its own database tables lets new cashiers rehearse against realistic data without touching production.

A public storefront for approved contractors: any visitor can browse the catalogue, but pricing, cart, and checkout only unlock after manual approval. Once logged in, every contractor sees their own negotiated price list, resolved at render time against an email × SKU lookup.

Checkout offers pickup at any of eight branch warehouses across Peninsular Malaysia. Completed orders are posted downstream to the Alaya ERP for inventory and accounting, and every invoice, quotation, and delivery order comes out as a PDF.

Approval workflow

Sign up → docs → review → live

Multi-step signup with document upload, then an admin holding page. Guest browsing stays open the whole time to keep the funnel warm — but pricing, cart, and checkout only unlock once a contractor is approved. The gate lives at the auth block in test-connection.php.

Custom pricing engine

Every contractor sees their own price

A CSV lookup of email × SKU overrides list price at render time in customprice.php and getprice.php. No schema changes needed to onboard a new client with a bespoke price list — drop new rows into the sheet and reload. Contractor tiers, one-off discounts, and negotiated project rates all use the same mechanism.

Payments & ERP sync

Billplz FPX → Alaya ERP

Billplz hosted redirect with server-to-server callback (billplz_callback.php) and a pending-order file store to survive dropped requests. A separate training-mode toggle points Billplz at sandbox for safe end-to-end testing. Every successful order posts to api.irsbizsuite.com.my with branch-to-warehouse/bin mapping, so accounting and inventory update without manual entry.

Documents & affiliate

PDFs, referrals, and preorders

Dompdf-generated invoices, quotations, and delivery orders, each keyed to order metadata. Sales-page traffic is captured through ?ref= and ?stid= URL params, tracked on a dedicated affiliate dashboard, and attributed to the salesperson who brought the contractor in. A separate preorder flow covers items not in immediate stock — own form, own payment path, own tracking screen.

Staff POS mode

Cashier-style ordering for in-branch staff

Sales staff at any of the eight branches log into staff_login.php and place orders through pos.php using the same catalogue as the storefront — branch selector at the top, contractor pricing applied at checkout, receipt printed at the counter.

Multi-branch fulfilment

Pick up where the stock actually is

Eight warehouses across Peninsular Malaysia — WHKP, STPK, SA15, IPOH, MSAI, SNWG, SKEM, KUTN — each with its own real-time stock read. Contractors choose their pickup branch at checkout and the fulfilment stub is created against the right warehouse in Alaya.

From order to doorstep, without another tab

Attach courier and tracking numbers inline against any order. Print delivery notes straight from the table. Filter by branch, fulfilment type, or salesperson. Auth & audit

Bcrypt login, activity log, role gates

Bcrypt admin auth against the admin_users Supabase table with password_verify. Legacy seed hashes auto-upgrade on first use, and the default seed forces a password rotation on first login. Every sign-in and mutation is logged through Supabase::logActivity for audit — useful when someone asks who changed what, and when.
